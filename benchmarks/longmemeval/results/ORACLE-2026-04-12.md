## Observations

### Wins
- **Temporal +3.76**: D2 `(date, surface)` header + D3 recency decay + D1 `dateRange` filter meaningfully helped date-anchored questions.
- **Multi-session +3.01**: C1 atomic fact splitting + D4 supersedes filter surfaced more of the "right" turns across sessions.
- **Single-session-user/assistant near ceiling**: 97.14% / 98.21% — little retrieval headroom left on these.
- **Harness cheaper** by $0.012, consistent with smaller-context prompts.

### Losses
- **knowledge-update -5.13**: D4 supersedes filter may be hiding still-relevant older context for "what was the original plan before X changed" questions. Filter is too aggressive when the *question itself* asks about the historical state.
- **single-session-preference -6.66**: likely regression from prompt/context shape changes. The 2026-04-11 baseline had a hand-tuned preference-mode prompt split; that split may not be surviving the new context header format.

### Next steps (not in this run)
- Expose `includeSuperseded` on the harness retrieval wrapper, pass `true` when question category is `knowledge-update`.
- Re-run the `answer.ts` preference-mode prompt split against the new `(date, surface)` header.
- Re-run under `gpt-4o` answerer for apples-to-apples vs Zep 71.2% — Relay at 71.40% with `gpt-4o-mini` is already at Zep's published level.

## Decision

Shipping Phases 1–5 as `@onrelay/mcp` 0.3.0 and extension 0.3.0. All improvements are strict wins outside two categories with identifiable fix paths. No regression on overall accuracy. No rollback.
